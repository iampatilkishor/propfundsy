## Two active risk flags worth reading before the rest of this list

Most futures-firm roundups just rank on price and split. Two firms on this list currently carry a live trust issue that's easy to miss if you're only comparing numbers:

**Topstep** — despite the strongest track record here (15 years, $1.5B+ paid), a 2026 federal lawsuit alleges deceptive changes to Live Program rules and payout caps cut by roughly 50–60% in April 2026; none of this is addressed on Topstep's own site, so it's sourced from trader and legal reporting only, not confirmed directly. Separately, Topstep disclosed a data breach on August 4, 2026 involving names, SSNs, dates of birth and medical information, reported to the Illinois and Texas Attorneys General. Neither issue means the firm is non-functional — traders are still getting funded and paid — but both are current and unresolved, and belong in the decision alongside the 15-year history.

**Alpha Futures** — carries a `caution` trust flag in our data because its Premium plan closed on July 12, 2026, with pending payouts converted to refunds rather than paid out as originally structured, and the firm lost its NinjaTrader/Tradovate integrations while migrating to an in-house platform. Its remaining Zero plan still functions, but this is a firm mid-transition, not a stable pick right now.

Everything else on this list carries a normal `ok` trust flag in our tracking.

## Our bottom line

For an established, high-trust default: **BluSky Trading Company** (5.0 Trustpilot, daily payouts) or **MyFundedFutures** (4.9, no activation fees) are the cleanest picks with no open flags. For cheapest meaningful entry, **NexGen ProTrader Funding**'s $29.80/mo promo on a $25K evaluation is hard to beat on price alone. If same-day money matters more than anything else, **Lucid Trading**, **TradeDay**, **YRM Prop** and **OneUp Trader** all run 1-day payout cycles on at least one tracked plan. And if you were about to fund with Topstep on reputation alone, read the lawsuit and data-breach disclosures first — the track record is real, but so are the two open issues.
