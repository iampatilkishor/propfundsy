Did **not** invent a platforms value for EverFunded's data or add a hard validator rule for it this run, since I can't confirm the firm's actual platform (its site wasn't in reach via search) and don't want to either fabricate a fact or turn this run's own validation step red over a gap I can't close. Recommend the maintainer fill in EverFunded's `platforms` via the normal `DATA_GUIDE.md` workflow, and consider adding `platforms`/`country` as required in `validate-data.mjs` once it's filled in, so a future firm added without it fails loudly instead of crashing comparison pages.

## 3. SEO content opportunities

1. **"Prop firms that shut down" tracker — carried over for the third time, still unbuilt, now with much stronger sourcing.** Flagged in the last two reports as the top content gap. This month's research turned up specific, citable 2026 casualties: True Forex Funds (ceased operations, ~300 traders, ~$1.2M left unpaid), FundingTicks (wound down May 2026 after a retroactive-profit-cut backlash), Seacrest Funding and MyFundedFX (both closed February 2026), on top of an industry-wide estimate of 80+ firms shut down since 2023 (~13–14% of all operators). Propfundsy's own dataset now includes several firms carrying real caution flags this run (QT Funded's collapsed Trustpilot score, NYS Markets' delisting, Orion's payout-denial pattern) — a shutdown/status tracker would tie directly into content the site is already building trust-flag-by-trust-flag, and would let `trustFlag`/`trustNote` do double duty as the tracker's data source. This is the strongest candidate precisely because it keeps not getting built; recommend actually scoping it next run.

2. **"Prop firms that allow EAs / algorithmic trading" landing page — new idea, near-zero build cost.** 2026 industry commentary (per this month's research) specifically calls out automation and EA-driven trading as a rising interest area among funded traders. Propfundsy already tracks this exactly: the `ea` tri-state field exists on every plan, and 22 of the site's 63 firms currently have at least one EA-allowed plan. This is almost pure data assembly — a filtered, sortable page in the same vein as the existing `instant-funding-prop-firms-2026.md` and `fastest-payout-prop-firms-2026.md` posts, no new data collection required.

3. **"Prop firm trader taxes" guide — new idea, genuine content gap.** None of the site's 13 existing blog posts touch tax treatment, and this run's research found real, dated, citable 2026 material to anchor a post on: the Form 1099-NEC reporting threshold rising from $600 to $2,000 under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act starting in 2026, Trader Tax Status (Section 475) eligibility, the 15.3% self-employment tax on net payouts, and deductibility of evaluation fees/data/software/home-office costs. This is squarely in the site's existing "help traders make an informed decision" lane (next to `prop-firm-pass-rates-2026.md` and `choosing-prop-firm-2026.md`) and doesn't depend on `firms.json`/`plans.json` data at all, so it's buildable independent of data-quality work. One caution: tax content should carry a clear "not tax advice, consult a professional" disclaimer given how fact-specific (LLC election, TTS qualification) the actual treatment gets per trader.

**Content shipped since last report, for context:** none of the last report's three recommendations (futures landing page, fastest-payout content, shutdown tracker) are brand new this run — the first two already shipped before this run started (`best-futures-prop-firms-2026.md`, `fastest-payout-prop-firms-2026.md` both exist), confirming last report's note that they'd landed. The shutdown tracker (#3 last time, #1 again this time) remains the one idea that keeps getting recommended and not built.
