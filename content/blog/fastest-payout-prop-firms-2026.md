"Fast payouts" is on nearly every prop firm's homepage. The number that actually matters is `payoutDays` — how many days typically pass between hitting a payout request and having the money — and most marketing pages don't state it plainly. We track that field on every plan in our dataset. Fifteen firms currently have at least one plan at 1 day or less: same-day, on-demand, or processed within hours of a request.

The rest are third-party sourced: real claims, but not independently confirmed, so treat the payout-days number as "reported" rather than guaranteed until you've checked the firm's current terms yourself.

## Speed and trust don't always move together

Two of the fifteen — **FunderPro** and **Blue Guardian** — carry a `caution` trust flag in our data alongside their fast-payout claims. FunderPro's ~8-hour average is backed by a reported $21.5M paid out in 2025, but it also sits under an active Trustpilot Consumer Warning, with a recurring complaint pattern of payouts flagged and rejected over "shared IP" or "unauthorized access" issues. **NYS Markets** is the one exception worth naming directly — it's both `verified: true` on its on-demand claim and carries a `caution` flag, which is a genuinely unusual combination and worth reading the firm's trust note for before funding.

The inverse pattern shows up too: **Hola Prime** is the only firm on this list with a payout claim backed by independent, third-party audit rather than a self-reported number — Deloitte reviewed its payout processing from October 2025 through March 2026 and found 98.35% of payouts cleared within an hour, with zero denials across the period. That's a meaningfully different quality of evidence than "24-hour guarantee, claimed."

One notable near-miss: **[TradersYard](/firms/tradersyard)** advertises a 48-hour payout guarantee (with a $500 penalty to the firm if missed) rather than same-day, so it doesn't make this list on paper — but trader reports describe actual payouts processing in under 4 hours, faster than several firms that do claim same-day. The guarantee is a floor, not the typical experience; worth knowing if a stated number is quietly ruling out a firm that's actually faster in practice.

## What "same-day" actually means in practice

Three different things get called "fast payouts," and they're not equivalent:

- **On-demand** (TradeDay, YRM Prop, Hola Prime, Upcomers, NYS Markets) — you can request a payout any time once eligible, and it's processed within hours rather than on a fixed cycle.
- **Daily, on a schedule** (Tradeify, BluSky, Shark Funded) — payouts run automatically on a recurring daily or weekday cadence once you're funded, rather than by request.
- **"Same-day" as a claimed ceiling, not a guarantee** (Take Profit Trader, Lucid Trading, TradersLaunch) — the firm states same-day processing, but unlike Hola Prime's audited figure or TradeDay's on-demand model, there's no independent number backing exactly how consistently that holds.

## Our bottom line

If independently verified speed is the priority, **Hola Prime**'s Deloitte-audited on-demand payouts are the strongest evidence-backed claim here, and **Tradeify**, **Shark Funded**, **NYS Markets** and **Upcomers** are the four payout-speed claims we've confirmed directly rather than sourced third-party. If you're chasing the fastest raw number, don't skip the trust flag: FunderPro and Blue Guardian's fast payouts come with an active Trustpilot warning attached, and that's exactly the kind of tradeoff a headline "24-hour payouts!" banner won't tell you.
